About Our Academy

Defence Career Academy, Krishna Nagar, Lucknow, Uttar Pradesh, has been a cornerstone of defence education since its establishment in 2008. For over 17 years, the academy has been dedicated to shaping the futures of aspiring candidates who aim to serve in the Indian Armed Forces and various paramilitary organizations. With a strong reputation and a commitment to excellence, the academy has become a trusted name in defence coaching in Lucknow.

Our Mission

The mission of Defence Career Academy is to empower students with the knowledge, skills, and confidence required to excel in the highly competitive examinations for the Indian Armed Forces. The academy strives to foster a disciplined and focused learning environment, enabling candidates to achieve their dreams of serving the nation.

Our Vision

Our vision is to be a leading institution that consistently produces successful candidates who uphold the values of duty, honor, and patriotism in their defence careers.
